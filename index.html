Pass-catching backs

Sportsbooks post a receiving line for only three running backs, so this comes from our own projections instead — every back, summed across the season. Under PPR a reception is worth as much as ten rushing yards, so two backs with the same rushing workload are not the same player at all. This is also the matchup-sensitive group: receiving backs measured 4.6× more responsive to the opponent than early-down grinders, which is the reverse of the usual story about bruisers and stout fronts.
